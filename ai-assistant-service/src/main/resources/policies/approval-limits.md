# Approval Limits (version 2026.1)

## Purchase and expense approval
- Up to 500.00: direct manager approves.
- 500.01 to 5,000.00: department head approves.
- 5,000.01 to 50,000.00: finance controller approves.
- Above 50,000.00: CFO approves.

## Budget changes
- Changes up to 10 percent of a budget: finance controller approves.
- Changes above 10 percent: CFO approves.
- Every change needs a written reason.

## Segregation of duties
The person who requests a payment cannot approve it.
