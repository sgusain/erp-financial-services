# Budget Rules (version 2026.1)

## Fiscal year
The fiscal year follows the calendar year. Budgets are created by finance before January 15.

## Status
A budget is ACTIVE while spending is below its limit. It becomes EXCEEDED when spending is above its limit. Finance reviews every EXCEEDED budget within 5 business days.

## Warnings
Budget owners are warned when spending reaches 90 percent of the limit.

## Overspending
Spending above the limit needs a budget change request with a reason. Spending is not blocked automatically.
