# Expense Policy (version 2026.1)

## Who can spend
All employees can submit expenses for business purposes only. Personal expenses are never reimbursed.

## Receipts
Every expense above 25.00 needs a receipt. Expenses without a receipt are rejected unless the finance team approves an exception in writing.

## Submission deadline
Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase date. Late expenses need approval from the department head.

## Travel
Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Hotels must be booked through the company travel desk. The daily meal allowance is 60.00.

## Software
Software subscriptions must be approved by IT before purchase. Annual plans need finance approval.
